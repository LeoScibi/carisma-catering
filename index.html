Tools

Sheet Setup
Creates the required tabs and header rows in your spreadsheet automatically. Run this first.
Ingredients
Generic ingredient master list — no pricing here, just name, category, and how it's used: weight, volume, or unit.
Suppliers
Your supplier list — name and contact details.
Price Lists
Links an ingredient to a supplier with pack size, pack unit, and price. This is where cost lives.
Recipes
Build recipes from ingredients and other recipes (sub-recipes). Costs to the cheapest supplier by default, with manual override per line.

How the data fits together

After saving your config and signing in, go to Setup to auto-create the five tabs this app needs: Ingredients, Suppliers, SupplierIngredients, Recipes, and RecipeLines. Ingredients are generic and carry no cost; pricing lives on SupplierIngredients, since the same ingredient can cost differently depending on the supplier. A recipe can include ingredients or other recipes as sub-recipe components — both convert through the same weight, volume, or unit family, metric only. Sign-in lasts about an hour — if a page shows an auth error, come back here and sign in again.
